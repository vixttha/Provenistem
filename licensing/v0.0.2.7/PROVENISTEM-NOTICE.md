For original, licensable expression in new contributions after the MIT baseline `4b57d194e8f79351dd41a548e7c0a574c97ecb94`, in the paths listed in [scope.json](scope.json), the rights holder would offer the unmodified [PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0](PROVENISTEM-LICENSE.md), only to the extent of rights actually held. The MIT license remains operative for the present package. No claim of exclusive ownership of all content is made.

## Proposed supplemental installation permission

For purposes permitted under PolyForm Strict 1.0.0, the licensor would additionally permit recipients to copy and retain the unchanged, officially published, revision-pinned Provenistem package in the dedicated Provenistem branch of a repository they control, whether private or public; operate it there; retain backups and archive copies for the same purpose; and replace it with another unchanged officially published revision. Include the applicable license and notice files and preserve all prior and third-party notices. For a public installation this permission would include hosting that unchanged package in the dedicated branch and the viewing and forking rights required by the hosting terms. A recipient's subsequent use remains subject to applicable licenses and law. This supplemental permission would not grant general redistribution, modification, sublicensing, resale or commercial-use rights beyond rights independently available under other applicable licenses or law.

Normal use includes creating, editing, exporting and retaining adopter-owned ideas, features, decisions, evidence references and generated indexes, as well as host-owned documentation and independently authored adapters. These records and outputs are not automatically subject to the restricted software terms. This permission does not authorize overwriting any host file, replacing a host license, modifying a project without owner authorization, granting trademark rights, or treating a review branch as an approved release.
